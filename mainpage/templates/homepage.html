University Identity

Vision

A premier, multidisciplinary technological university.

Mission

The University shall primarily provide advanced professional and technical instruction for special purposes, advanced studies in various fields, and also undertake research and extension services.

Goals

The University shall produce scientifically and technologically oriented human capital equipped with appropriate knowledge, skills, and attitudes. It shall also pursue relevant research and strengthen linkages.

Office of Student Affairs (SAO)

The Office of Student Affairs (SAO), also referred to as the Office of Student Affairs and Services (OSAS) on campus, is the primary office dedicated to managing and coordinating student welfare, development, and discipline. It serves as the central hub for most non-academic student services, aiming to support students' holistic development in line with the university's mission.

The Student Affairs Office coordinates a wide range of services to support students throughout their academic journey. Below are some of the key services available to students at the Argao campus. Click on any service to learn more.

The Guidance Office provides essential services starting from admission. They manage entrance examination applications and offer personal counseling, group testing, and career guidance to help students navigate academic and personal challenges.

Contact: Michaella Angela G. Bajenting (Guidance Services Coordinator)

Email: guidance.argao@ctu.edu.ph

In line with the university's goal of providing access to education for the underprivileged, the SAO manages and disseminates information about various scholarship programs. These include academic scholarships, government-funded grants (like CHED and DOST), and private scholarships.

The SAO oversees the accreditation and activities of all campus student organizations, including the Supreme Student Council (SSC). This service fosters leadership, community engagement, and a vibrant campus life through various academic, cultural, and social activities. As of 2020, there were 14 accredited student organizations.

SSC Office Email: supremestudentcouncil85@gmail.com

The campus provides for the well-being of its students through the KAHIMSUG Center, which functions as the medical and dental clinic. It handles first-aid, medical check-ups, and health-related concerns.

While a distinct office, the Registrar is a key student service point, managing student records, enrollment, graduations, and the issuance of official documents like Transcripts of Records (TOR) and certifications.
