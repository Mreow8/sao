TECHNOCRATS LOGO SYMBOLISM

The logo of the Bachelor of Industrial Technology program at CTU Argao Campus is more than just a design; it's a rich tapestry of symbolism reflecting the diverse majors within the program.

Each element within the logo carries specific meaning, representing different aspects of industrial technology education. Let's delve into the symbolism encapsulated in this emblematic representation and understand how it embodies the comprehensive education and dynamic spirit fostered by the department.

View CBL List of Officers List of Advisers

Key elements of the logo include:

  • The Suit: Represents Garments Technology, showing professionalism and skill in making clothes.
  • The Graduation Cap: Shaped like a computer monitor, it stands for Computer Technology, highlighting the importance of knowing how to use computers and technology.
  • Interconnected Lines: Coming from the computer, these represent Electronics Technology, showing innovation and how things connect together.
  • The Spanner (Wrench) as a Necktie: Symbolizes Automotive Technology, representing precision and engineering skills.
  • Technical Pen: Below the spanner, it represents Drafting Technology, showing accuracy and design skills.

The logo shows a student, representing the well-rounded education and different skills students learn in the program. It highlights how students are prepared for various industrial jobs.
